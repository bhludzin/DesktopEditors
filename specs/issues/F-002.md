## What differs

Punctuation and digits (Unicode script Common) were shaped as separate runs from the surrounding Latin letters, so kerning pairs such as letter+period or letter+comma were never applied.

## What PowerPoint does

Kerns across letter/punctuation pairs within a run.

## What Euro-Office does

Itemized text by script and split at every Latin/Common boundary.

## Where to look

common/libfont/textshaper.js (private_IsCommonScriptJoin, keepScript): Common-script punctuation and digits join the surrounding LTR run (UAX #24).
