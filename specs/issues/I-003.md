# I-003: Space after one paragraph and space before the next are collapsed to the larger value instead of added

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | fixed (fixed in sdkjs 42d5db5758 (fix(slide): space paragraphs and blank lines like PowerPoint)) |
| Severity | major |
| Slides | 6 |
| Findings | 6 |
| Categories | text-line-spacing |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Some paragraphs carry both spcBef and spcAft. Between two such paragraphs Euro-Office applies only one spacing. Slides 800/801/808/809 have numbered 24 pt headings with spcBef 13.5 pt and spcAft 15 pt. There every paragraph boundary loses about 15.6 px, so heading 2/3/4 rise 15.5/31/46.5 px and print over the separately positioned quote boxes (major overlap). In the PDF, PowerPoint's baseline pitch is 57.87 pt and Euro-Office's is 43.80 pt (28.8 pt line + 15 pt). On slides 569 and 624 (spcBef = spcAft = 4.5 pt) each boundary loses about 6 px.

## What PowerPoint does

The gap between paragraphs is spcAft(previous) + spcBef(next), each rounded as in vertical/3. Slide 800: 28.8 pt line + 15 + 14 = 57.8 pt (measured 57.87). Slide 569: 16.2 + 5 + 5 = 26.2 pt (measured 26.16).

## What Euro-Office does

The gap is max(spcAft(previous), spcBef(next)): 28.8 + 15 = 43.8 pt on slide 800, and 16.2 + 4.5 = 20.7 pt on slide 569.

## Suspected cause

Word's paragraph-spacing merge is applied to slide text: Paragraph.Get_CompiledPr sets the current paragraph's Spacing.Before to max(Prev_After, Cur_Before) - Prev_After, whatever the document type.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph.js: Paragraph.prototype.Get_CompiledPr (10734); the line 'Pr.ParaPr.Spacing.Before = Math.max(Prev_After, Cur_Before) - Prev_After;' (10836).

## Fix idea

When !this.bFromDocument (presentation text body), skip the max-merge and keep Pr.ParaPr.Spacing.Before = Cur_Before (CalculateBefore()), so that the previous paragraph's After and this paragraph's Before add up. Leave the first-paragraph (Before = 0) and last-paragraph (After = 0) presentation rules as they are; they match PowerPoint in this deck.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
