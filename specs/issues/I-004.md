# I-004: No line break allowed at a space before '.', so spaced ellipses ('word . . .') are glued to the preceding word

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | major |
| Slides | 5 |
| Findings | 5 |
| Categories | text-wrap |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

5 boxes on 5 slides (35, 257, 594, 613, 755), all plain 13.5 pt Calibri quote text containing a spaced ellipsis written with ordinary U+0020 spaces ('slain . . .', 'principles.  . . .', 'CE.  . . .', 'thinkers.  . . .', 'me.  . . . “'). PowerPoint breaks at one of the spaces inside the ellipsis, so a line may end '... slain . .' and the next start with '. and he beheld'. Euro-Office treats word + all dots as one unbreakable unit; when the unit does not fit, the preceding word moves to the next line even though it fits with 5-70 px to spare. Every following line of the paragraph then reflows (8-14 lines differ); on 594 the spAutoFit box gains a line and its last word 'to' prints over the text of Rectangle 7. Not a borderline width effect: EO's own advances on these lines are equal or narrower than PowerPoint's (K2).

## What PowerPoint does

Latin text breaks at any space. A line may begin with '.', ',', ';', ')' etc. when the preceding character is a space (no UAX #14 LB13 'x IS' rule, no kinsoku for Latin script).

## What Euro-Office does

A '.' that follows a space is not a break opportunity: the space before it is folded into the word and the last break position stays before the preceding word, so 'slain . . .' (about 50 px) must fit as a whole.

## Suspected cause

CRunText.CanBeAtBeginOfLine returns false for every character flagged PUNCTUATION_FLAG_CANT_BE_AT_BEGIN in g_aPunctuation ('.', ',', ':', ';', '!', '?', ')', ']', '%' ...). In ParaRun.Recalculate_Range, a word that starts with such a character does not call PRS.Set_LineBreakPos (else-branch near line 3986), and if it does not fit it jumps back to the previous break position (MoveToLBP, line 3951); with PRS.LineBreakFirst it is merged into the preceding text (line 3973). This kinsoku-style rule is applied to Latin text even when a space precedes the character.

## Where to look

word/Editor/Run.js: ParaRun.prototype.Recalculate_Range (3641) - word-start branch lines 3947-3990 (3951 MoveToLBP, 3973 LineBreakFirst merge, 3986 Set_LineBreakPos only if CanBeAtBeginOfLine; also the isBreakBefore test at 3843); word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Text.js: CRunText.prototype.CanBeAtBeginOfLine (572); flags in common/commonDefines.js g_aPunctuation (2256-2352).

## Fix idea

In Recalculate_Range treat a character that follows a space (SpaceLen > 0 / previous item is para_Space) as a valid break position when the character is not East Asian: e.g. let canBegin = Item.CanBeAtBeginOfLine() || (SpaceLen > 0 && !AscCommon.isEastAsianScript(Item.Value)); and use canBegin at lines 3951, 3973 and 3986. Restrict to presentations (Asc.editor.isPresentationEditor) first if Word documents must not change, though Word also allows this for Latin text. Keep the rule for no-space cases ('word.' must stay glued) and for East Asian punctuation (kinsoku).
