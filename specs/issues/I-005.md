# I-005: Kerning pairs that involve the space glyph are never applied (Times New Roman 'space A', 'space T')

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | major |
| Slides | 1 |
| Findings | 1 |
| Categories | text-glyph-spacing |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Slide 1's 96 pt Times New Roman title line 'Of All Things' (spc=-300, kern=1200 from the master title style) is 7.5 px wider in Euro-Office. The gap 'Of'->'A' is 29 vs 23 px and 'All'->'Things' is 32.5 vs 31 px, so 'Of' sits 4 px left and 'Things' 3.5 px right on the centred line. times.ttf has kern pairs space+A = -113 and space+T = -37 units (-5.89 and -1.93 px at 96 pt, -7.81 px in total), which matches the measured 7.5 px. The first title line, which has no space pairs, matches exactly. Calibri has no space pairs, so the Calibri body text is unaffected, but Times New Roman and Arial text (both have space pairs) will show this whenever kern applies.

## What PowerPoint does

Applies the font's pair kerning across spaces (space+A, space+T, A+space and so on) when the run size is at or above the kern threshold.

## What Euro-Office does

Flushes the shaping buffer at every space and measures the space on its own with its plain advance, so no kern pair with a space as either glyph is ever applied.

## Suspected cause

CParagraphTextShaper.HandleRun treats a space (a non-text item) as a word boundary: it calls FlushWord() and then private_HandleSpace, which takes the bare glyph advance. HarfBuzz never sees the (prev, space) or (space, next) pairs.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph/TextShaper.js: CParagraphTextShaper.HandleRun (141, the `!oItem.IsText()` branch that does FlushWord + private_HandleSpace) and private_HandleSpace (293)

## Fix idea

When the run is kerned (TextPr.IsKerned()), add the kern pairs on both sides of the space to the space's width. Look up kern(prevGlyph, space) and kern(space, nextGlyph) from the font (HarfBuzz hb_font_get_glyph_h_kerning, or by shaping a 3-glyph string prev+space+next and taking the space's advance delta). Fold both into the space item's width in private_HandleSpace. Keeping spaces as separate items preserves line breaking and justification. The same lookup is needed at run boundaries where the font stays the same.
