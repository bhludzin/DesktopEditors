# I-008: When no line join is specified (in a:ln or the theme line style), Euro-Office strokes with a miter join; PowerPoint uses a round join

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | fixed (fixed in sdkjs 7937b6f01f (fix(slide): stroke outlines with PowerPoint's line join)) |
| Severity | minor |
| Slides | 18 |
| Findings | 18 |
| Categories | shape-fill-line |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

18 findings on 18 slides (187-193, 201-206, 243, 585, 676, 679, 701). Affects every outline whose a:ln and referenced theme lnStyleLst entry have no <a:round/>, <a:bevel/> or <a:miter/>. theme1 has no join in any line style, and the only explicit join anywhere in the slide XML is <a:miter/> (189 occurrences). On rectangles it shows as square, filled outer corners where PowerPoint draws an arc of radius w/2: the thick red 3 pt and 4.5 pt frames on 187-193 (1.7-2.5 sheet px radius), and the 1.5-3 pt frames on 243 and 585 (trivial); see crop grouping/work-outlines/0188-corner.png. On acute vertices it shows as miter spikes. The yellow leftArrow heads (1.5 pt) on 201-206 get back-corner spikes about 1 px further out, and the tip moves 0.5 px. The rtTriangle with a 4.5 pt line on 676/679 gets a tip 2.5-4 px beyond PowerPoint's rounded corner. Each of the eleven foldedCorner fold vertices on 701 (2 pt, 31-degree angle) gets a 2.5 px barb. Line widths and positions match.

## What PowerPoint does

An outline whose join is not given (in its a:ln or in the theme line style its lnRef names) is stroked with a round join: the PDF writes '1 j' before the stroke. That holds for 156 of the 187 presets, custom geometry, connectors (straight, bent and curved), text boxes, shapes in groups and picture outlines. The other 31 presets (bentUpArrow, chevron, cube, downArrow, ellipseRibbon, ellipseRibbon2, leftArrowCallout, leftRightArrowCallout, leftUpArrow, mathEqual, mathMinus, mathMultiply, mathNotEqual, mathPlus, moon, notchedRightArrow, plus, quadArrowCallout, rightArrow, star4, star5, star8, star10, star12, star16, star24, star32, stripedRightArrow, upArrow, upDownArrow and upDownArrowCallout) are stroked with a miter join, also when the XML says <a:round/>; flipping, rotating, the line width (0.75-6 pt), a dash, no fill, a text box or a group do not change that. <a:bevel/> gives a bevel join ('2 j') and <a:miter/> a miter join (no 'j') on every preset. The miter limit is never written (the PDF default, 10), whatever a:miter/@lim says (800000 and 100000 tested); a theme line style with <a:miter lim="800000"/> gives a miter join. Lines with arrowheads are filled outlines in the PDF, not strokes; their bent shafts have round corners unless the join is miter. Measured on two test decks built for it: linejoin-rules (all 187 presets with no join, 6 presets with 8 join variants, 32 other cases) and linejoin-rules2 (the 31 mitered presets with <a:round/> and <a:bevel/>, 16 round ones with <a:miter/> and <a:bevel/>, 28 lines and connectors with arrowheads).

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: every stroke in its PDF had a miter join ('0 J 0 j 10 M'), an explicit round or bevel join included. On screen an outline with no join was mitered (the canvas default); an explicit join was used. On the 838-slide deck 1518 of the strokes paired with PowerPoint's were mitered where PowerPoint's are round, on 239 slides; the 268 strokes PowerPoint miters matched.

## Suspected cause

Confirmed: ShapeDrawer used the join only when the a:ln or the theme line style gave one, so an unspecified join fell through to the canvas or PDF default (miter), and the PDF branch of drawFillStroke never passed a join to the writer, because OldLineJoin is only set when there is a canvas context. PowerPoint's per-preset exception (31 presets mitered even when the join is round) was not known when the issue was opened; the rightArrow on slides 207 and 701 was the one case seen.

## Where to look

word/Drawing/ShapeDrawer.js: CShapeDrawer.checkSlideLineJoin, called from draw with the geometry being drawn, and SLIDE_MITER_JOIN_PRESETS; ds uses LineJoinType for the canvas, and drawFillStroke's PDF branch passes it to the writer with put_PenLineJoin and puts the writer back to miter after the path.

## Fix idea

Both changes are needed, or the PDF output stays mitered. (a) In CShape.prototype.recalculatePen, after pen.merge, if this.pen.Join is null (or its type is null), set it to a LineJoin of type AscFormat.LineJoinType.Round (or treat null as Round inside ShapeDrawer). (b) In ShapeDrawer, decouple the PDF path from m_oContext: compute joinType in fromShape regardless of the graphics object, save and restore m_oContext.lineJoin only when a context exists, and in drawFillStroke's PDF branch always call this.Graphics.put_PenLineJoin(AscFormat.ConvertJoinAggType(joinType)), plus the miter limit when a:miter lim is given. Afterwards check: slide 188 (round frame corners); slides 201, 676, 679 and 701 (spikes gone); slide 141 (explicit miter stays miter); and the rightArrow on 207 and 701, which PowerPoint miters and may need a preset-specific exception.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
