# I-009: Point-based paragraph spacing (spcPts) is applied exactly; PowerPoint rounds it to whole points (4.5 pt becomes 5 pt)

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | fixed (fixed in sdkjs 42d5db5758 (fix(slide): space paragraphs and blank lines like PowerPoint)) |
| Severity | minor |
| Slides | 18 |
| Findings | 18 |
| Categories | text-line-spacing |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Every paragraph boundary that carries spcAft or spcBef spcPts val=450 is about 0.5 pt (0.5-0.6 px) shorter in Euro-Office. This holds with or without bullets, with buAutoNum, and after an 18 pt heading. The shortfall accumulates down a list: 4.1 px after 7 boundaries in the 'Jezebel' boxes (542-544, 547), 1-1.5 px on 436/446/676/679, and 0.4-0.6 px for a single boundary (246-248, 443, 562, 837). The 84 spcPts=450 values are the commonest spacing in the deck. PDF baselines confirm it: bullet pitch on 542 is 21.14-21.24 pt in PowerPoint vs 20.70 pt in Euro-Office, and on 710/837 the spcBef gaps are 21.24/21.27 vs 20.70 pt. Whole-point values match exactly: 6 pt spcBef on slide 508 (463.31 vs 463.27 px) and 9 pt on slides 244/246. Percentage spacing is not rounded by PowerPoint: the spcBef 20% on 579 gives 3.6 pt in both.

## What PowerPoint does

A spcPts value appears to be rounded to whole points before layout: 4.5 -> 5.0 pt (gap 16.2 + 5.0 = 21.2 pt), 13.5 -> 14 pt (slide 800: 28.8 + 15 + 14 = 57.8 pt, measured 57.87), while 6, 9 and 15 pt are unchanged. The deck holds only .5 fractions, so round-half-up and ceil cannot be told apart.

## What Euro-Office does

Uses the exact value, 4.5 pt = 5.0 px at 800 px, so each such boundary is 0.5 pt short.

## Suspected cause

Serialize.js converts spcPts val (1/100 pt) to mm exactly (Pts*0.00352777778) and layout uses it unchanged. PowerPoint quantizes point spacing to whole points.

## Where to look

sdkjs/common/Shapes/Serialize.js: ReadTextParagraphPr (9567); spcAft Pts at 9727 and spcBef Pts at 9762 ('para_pr.Spacing.After/Before = Pts*0.00352777778'). The layout-side value is used in word/Editor/Paragraph.js Paragraph.prototype.Get_CompiledPr (10734), via CalculateBefore/CalculateAfter in word/Editor/Styles/para-spacing.js (311/320).

## Fix idea

For slide paragraphs only, round point-based Before/After to whole points at layout time, e.g. in Get_CompiledPr when !this.bFromDocument: v = Math.floor(v / g_dKoef_pt_to_mm + 0.5 + 1e-6) * g_dKoef_pt_to_mm, applied only when the value came from spcPts (BeforePct/AfterPct undefined). Do not round at import, so files keep 450 on save. Confirm half-up vs ceil with a test slide using 4.2 / 4.7 pt, and check whether lnSpc spcPts (Serialize.js 9689) is rounded the same way. Do this together with vertical/2, since slides 569/624/800 need both changes.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
