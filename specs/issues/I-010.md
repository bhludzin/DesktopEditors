# I-010: baseline="30000" superscripts use the generic Word raise (35%) and size (0.65, rounded to 11.5 pt) instead of the stated 30% and PowerPoint's 2/3 size

| | |

| Status | open |
| Severity | minor |
| Slides | 10 |
| Findings | 10 |
| Categories | text-position |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Ordinal superscripts ('th', 'rd') in 18 pt Calibri paragraphs are drawn about 0.6-0.85 px higher in Euro-Office, slightly narrower (about 0.5 px per 'th') and about 10-13% lighter. The narrower advance adds to line drift (-2.5 px at line end on 523). The PDF of slide 508 gives the exact values. PowerPoint draws 'th' at Tf 12.0 with the baseline raised 5.40 pt (135.74 -> 130.34). Euro-Office draws it at Tf 11.5 raised 6.30 pt (135.76 -> 129.46).

## What PowerPoint does

Raise = baseline/100000 x run size = 0.30 x 18 = 5.4 pt. The superscript is drawn at 2/3 of the run size (12.0 pt).

## What Euro-Office does

Ignores the percentage. It raises by AscCommon.vaKSuper (0.35) x 18 = 6.3 pt and scales by vaKSize 0.65, rounded to half points by AlignFontSize: 11.7 -> 11.5 pt.

## Suspected cause

The reader keeps only the sign of a:rPr/@baseline (VertAlign super/sub) and drops the value. Drawing and measuring then use the Word constants vaKSuper/vaKSize.

## Where to look

sdkjs/common/Shapes/Serialize.js: ReadRunProperties (8904), case 2 at 8933-8938 (baseline -> VertAlign only); sdkjs/common/commonDefines.js 790-792 (vaKSize 0.65, vaKSuper 0.35, vaKSub -0.141); word/Editor/Paragraph/draw/content-draw-state.js ParagraphContentDrawState.prototype.calculateY (716); word/Editor/Common.js AlignFontSize (560).

## Fix idea

Store the baseline percentage on the run's TextPr when reading pptx (for example a new BaselineShift field, or reuse Position in points computed at layout). For slide paragraphs, raise and lower by pct x FontSize in calculateY, in line-draw-state and in position-calculator, instead of vaKSuper/vaKSub. Use a 2/3 size factor without half-point rounding for pptx super/subscript, in measurement and drawing. Verify subscript baseline=-25000 the same way.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
