# I-011: Pair kerning is applied between a letter and a following hyphen-minus (T-, r-, t-), which PowerPoint does not kern

## What differs

In Euro-Office the hyphen in words like 'ASHURST-MCGEE' (Calibri Bold 18 pt), 'Latter-day' (regular 18 pt) and 'covenant-breaking' (regular 13.5 pt) sits closer to the letter before it. The step is 1.5 px for bold T+hyphen (T stem to hyphen gap 2.5 vs 4.0 px, slides 497, 498, 500 and 503 twice), 1.0 px for r+hyphen (511 and 513, where the hyphen touches the r) and 0.85 px for t+hyphen (567). Every glyph after the hyphen keeps the offset, so the rest of the line shows doubled edges and its drift passes 2 px when K2 adds to it. The hyphen-to-next-letter gap and the hyphen width match. The step sizes equal Calibri's own kern/GPOS pairs, which agree with each other: Calibri Bold T+hyphen = -140 units (-1.37 px at 18 pt), Calibri r+hyphen = -60 (-0.59 px at 18 pt), t+hyphen = -63 (-0.46 px at 13.5 pt). PowerPoint shows none of them. Hyphens after letters with no pair (o+hyphen in 'go-between', slide 508) show no step. Slides 499, 503 (URL) and 776/777 also contain such pairs, but their findings are mainly K2 drift and are grouped there.

## What PowerPoint does

Leaves the letter+hyphen-minus pair unkerned even with kern=1200 active, while it does kern letter+letter and letter+period/comma pairs (the earlier kerning fix confirmed 'y.' and 'r,'). The hyphen is placed at the unkerned advance of the preceding glyph.

## What Euro-Office does

Since the kern fix, HarfBuzz shapes each word with the kern feature. A hyphen-minus is appended to the current word buffer before the flush that its SpaceAfter flag triggers, so the pair (letter, hyphen) is kerned.

## Suspected cause

PowerPoint appears to treat U+002D (a break-after character) as the start of its own shaping segment, so no kern pair reaches into it. Euro-Office flushes the word only after the hyphen (CRunText.private_IsSpaceAfter marks 0x002D/0x2014), not before.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph/TextShaper.js: CParagraphTextShaper.HandleRun (141), at the `this.AppendToString(oItem); if (oItem.IsSpaceAfter()) this.FlushWord();` branch (171-174); hyphen flag in word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Text.js: CRunText.private_IsSpaceAfter (561)

## Fix idea

In HandleRun, when the item is a breaking hyphen (code point 0x002D with IsSpaceAfter()), call this.FlushWord() before AppendToString so the hyphen is shaped as its own segment. This drops the kern into the hyphen, and EO already drops the kern out of it. Check 'ASHURST-MCGEE' on 497: the T-to-hyphen gap should become 4.0 px. Also check that 'y.'/'r,' pairs keep their kerning. Consider the same treatment for U+2014, which also carries SpaceAfter.
