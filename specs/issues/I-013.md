# I-013: In boxes with wrap="none", trailing spaces are left out of the line width when centring or right-aligning, so such a line sits half a space (or a whole space) too far right

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | fixed (fixed in sdkjs 4c3273edbc (fix(slide): superscripts, hyphens, dashes and trailing spaces like PowerPoint)) |
| Severity | minor |
| Slides | 6 |
| Findings | 6 |
| Categories | text-position |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Centred labels in wrap=none spAutoFit boxes whose run text ends with a space ('Establish Zion (The Law Of Consecration) ', 'Bind Up The Testimony, ', 'Among The Gentiles ', ...) are shifted right as a whole. The shift is 2.4-2.85 px at 21 pt bold and 2.9-3.15 px at 24 pt bold, exactly half a Calibri Bold space. Lines of the same boxes without a trailing space match within 0.25 px. The box outlines do not move.

## What PowerPoint does

In a box with wrap="none", the trailing spaces are part of the line width for centred and right-aligned lines, on every line, including one that ends at an <a:br/>: the visible glyphs move left by half the trailing spaces when centred and by all of them when right-aligned. In a wrapped box they do not count, even when the text fits on one line (test deck dash-super-rules slide 6).

## What Euro-Office does

Centres only the visible part. Range.W excludes the trailing spaces, which are accumulated in PRSC.SpaceLen and never added.

## Suspected cause

Word's rule that trailing spaces hang past the margin (so they do not affect alignment) is applied to slide text.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph_Recalculate.js: Paragraph.prototype.private_RecalculateLineAlign (1897), align_Center case 'X = Range.X + (RangeWidth - Range.W) / 2' (2043), align_Right at 2026. Trailing-space accounting is in word/Editor/Run.js ParaRun.prototype.Recalculate_Range_Width (5233): para_Space adds to PRSC.SpaceLen, which is added to Range.W only when a following letter arrives.

## Fix idea

In private_RecalculateLineAlign, for slide paragraphs (!this.bFromDocument) and the range that contains the paragraph end (PRSC.ParaEnd), use Range.W + PRSC.SpaceLen as the occupied width for center (and right) alignment. Check separately whether PowerPoint also counts the breaking space on wrapped lines (none of these findings involve wrapping). Keep RTL's rtlShift logic consistent.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
