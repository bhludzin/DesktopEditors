# I-014: Outer shadow blurRad is ignored: the shadow is painted as a hard-edged flat silhouette

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | minor |
| Slides | 5 |
| Findings | 5 |
| Categories | shape-fill-line |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

5 findings on slides 186, 187, 194, 195 and 702; 11 slides in the deck use outerShdw with a non-zero blurRad. The shadow's offset and extent match; only the blur is missing. On slide 702 (picture on white; blurRad 4 pt, dist 3 pt at 45 degrees, 40% black) Euro-Office paints a flat band of luminance 153, exactly 2 sheet px wide, right of and below the frame. PowerPoint fades from 163 to 254 over about 6 px. The picture therefore looks as if it has a thick dark border on two sides (minor). On 186-195, the 50%-black text panel (Rectangle 4; blurRad 50800, dist 38100, dir 2700000, 40% black) casts its shadow onto a dark photo. There Euro-Office shows a flat band (luminance 35, 35, 35, 35, then a jump of 9-17 levels) where PowerPoint fades over about 4 px. That shows only in the difference map (trivial).

## What PowerPoint does

Draws the shadow as a blurred silhouette (blur radius blurRad = 50800 EMU = 4 pt, roughly 4-6 sheet px of fade), offset by dist/dir. In its PDF the shadow is a rasterized image XObject with a soft mask (page 702: /Image1813, 665x320, /SMask; page 186 has many SMask images).

## What Euro-Office does

Fills the offset silhouette with the shadow colour at its alpha ('0 0 0 rg /E2 gs f' with ca 0.4). Nothing is blurred, so the edge is hard. Its PDF of page 186 has no image XObjects.

## Suspected cause

recalculateShdw builds shdwSp as a plain CShape with the same geometry and the shadow fill, and drawShdw just draws it with the shadow transform. outerShdw.blurRad is read and serialized in Format.js but never used when drawing (sdkjs drawing has no shadowBlur or canvas filter anywhere).

## Where to look

sdkjs common/Drawings/Format/GraphicObjectBase.js: CGraphicObjectBase.prototype.recalculateShdw (line 1164) and CGraphicObjectBase.prototype.drawShdw (line 1310). Called from common/Drawings/Format/Shape.js draw (lines 5568, 5616) and common/Drawings/Format/Image.js: CImageShape.prototype.draw (line 606).

## Fix idea

When outerShdw.blurRad > 0, render the shadow silhouette into an offscreen RGBA buffer padded by blurRad and blur it (canvas ctx.filter = 'blur(sigma px)', or a 3-pass box blur). PowerPoint's blurRad is roughly the full fade width, so sigma is about blurRad/2 to blurRad/3 in device px; tune it against slide 702. Draw the result as an image at the shadow transform. In PDF export, emit it as an image with an alpha mask, as PowerPoint does. Keep the flat vector fill only for blurRad = 0. recalculateShdw's 12700 pen-width padding (line 1198) belongs to outlines/1.
