# I-017: With line spacing reduced to 80% by normAutofit lnSpcReduction, the first baseline sits about 0.35-0.4 pt too high

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | trivial |
| Slides | 4 |
| Findings | 4 |
| Categories | text-position |
| Confidence in the cause | low |

## What differs

Two boxes with an effective 80% line spacing are shifted up as a whole while the line pitch matches. One is the body placeholder on 579 (normAutofit fontScale=47500 lnSpcReduction=20000, 100% lnSpc; 16 lines, top-anchored). The other is the title text box on 594-596 (fontScale=85000 lnSpcReduction=10000 on lstStyle lnSpc 90%; one line, anchor ctr). PDF: slide 579 first baseline 142.39 pt in PowerPoint vs 142.00 in Euro-Office (-0.39 pt), every later line the same; slide 594 title 108.14 vs 107.79 (-0.35 pt). Scaled font sizes agree (15 pt and 28 pt in both PDFs), and at 100% spacing first baselines agree to 0.01 pt (e.g. slide 718).

## What PowerPoint does

At 15 pt Calibri and 80% spacing, the first baseline is 10.83 pt (0.722 em) below the text-area top, versus 0.936 em at 100%. The reduction above the first baseline is about 0.214 em.

## What Euro-Office does

The first baseline is 10.44 pt (0.696 em) below the top, 0.240 em = 20% x 1.2 em higher than at 100%. Euro-Office removes the full 20% of the 1.2 em line above the first baseline, while PowerPoint removes slightly less. On the 90%-10% title, EO uses 0.9 - 0.1 = 0.8; PowerPoint may combine them differently (0.9 x 0.9 = 0.81).

## Suspected cause

How a percentage line spacing below 100% is distributed over the first line, and possibly how lnSpc and lnSpcReduction are combined (subtractive in EO). Not confirmed.

## Where to look

Not located precisely. Candidates: word/Editor/Paragraph.js Get_CompiledPr2 area 11233-11239 ('Spacing.Line = Spacing.Line - Pr.ParaPr.LnSpcReduction'), and word/Editor/Paragraph_Recalculate.js CParaLineMetrics.Recalculate_LineGap (2980) / private_RecalculateLinePosition (1339) for the first-line ascent under linerule_Auto < 1.

## Fix idea

Build a small test deck (Calibri 15/28 pt; lnSpc 100/90/80% with and without lnSpcReduction) and compare PowerPoint's first baseline with EO's. Then change the first-line reduction for slide text to match: e.g. scale only the part of the line above the ascent, and decide between subtractive (lnSpc - reduction) and multiplicative (lnSpc x (1 - reduction)) combination.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
