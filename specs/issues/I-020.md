# I-020: A line that mixes fonts (Calibri text with an Arial run) grows by 0.58 pt above the baseline in Euro-Office; PowerPoint lowers the baseline by only 0.16 pt

## What differs

Found while fixing I-006, not by the review. Slide 674, Rectangle 5: line 1 holds 13.5 pt Calibri and, since I-006, a 13.5 pt Arial he-IL run. Baselines in the PDFs, in pt from the page top, for lines 1, 3 and 4: PowerPoint 382.01, 414.29, 430.49; Euro-Office before I-006 (all Calibri) 381.85, 414.25, 430.45; Euro-Office now 382.43, 414.83, 431.03. PowerPoint puts line 1's baseline 0.16 pt below where an all-Calibri line would have it, and every later line 0.04 pt lower: the mixed line is 0.04 pt taller, with 16.08 pt from its baseline to the next instead of 16.2. Euro-Office makes the mixed line 0.58 pt taller, all of it above the baseline, so the whole box's text sits 0.42-0.54 pt below PowerPoint's and the slide's SSIM fell 0.9807 -> 0.9494 with the I-006 fix.

## What PowerPoint does

Measured once: a 13.5 pt line that mixes Calibri and Arial is 0.04 pt taller than an all-Calibri line, with its baseline 0.16 pt lower. The rule is not known. The largest win ascent and descent are both Calibri's, which would change nothing. Splitting the 1.2 em line at each font's win ascent / descent ratio and taking the larger parts would give 0.49 pt.

## What Euro-Office does

CTextMetrics.Update keeps the largest ascent, descent and line gap of the line's fonts separately. Calibri (ascent 0.952, descent 0.269 em, no gap in Euro-Office's terms) and Arial (0.905, 0.212, gap 0.033 em) together give Calibri's ascent and descent plus Arial's gap, a line taller than either font makes. The gap alone is 0.44 pt at 13.5 pt; the line grows by 0.58 pt in the PDF.

## Suspected cause

Word's way of combining the metrics of the fonts on a line, applied to slides. Not confirmed, and PowerPoint's own rule still has to be measured.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Styles.js CTextMetrics.Update (about line 15344); the line metrics in ParaRun.Recalculate_LineMetrics and Paragraph.private_RecalculateLineMetrics; percentage line spacing in the presentation path of Paragraph line recalculation.

## Fix idea

Measure first: a test deck with lines of Calibri plus one run in another font (Arial, Times New Roman, Segoe UI, Cambria, a larger Calibri run) at 100%, 90% and 150% line spacing and two sizes, with the baselines read from PowerPoint's PDF. Then change, for slide text only, how the line's ascent, descent and gap are combined. Related to I-016 (Times New Roman first baseline) and I-017 (first baseline at other line spacings).

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
