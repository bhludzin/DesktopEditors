## What differs

Found while fixing I-006, not by the review. Slide 674, Rectangle 5: line 1 holds 13.5 pt Calibri and, since I-006, a 13.5 pt Arial he-IL run. Baselines in the PDFs, in pt from the page top, for lines 1, 3 and 4: PowerPoint 382.01, 414.29, 430.49; Euro-Office before I-006 (all Calibri) 381.85, 414.25, 430.45; Euro-Office now 382.43, 414.83, 431.03. PowerPoint puts line 1's baseline 0.16 pt below where an all-Calibri line would have it, and every later line 0.04 pt lower: the mixed line is 0.04 pt taller, with 16.08 pt from its baseline to the next instead of 16.2. Euro-Office makes the mixed line 0.58 pt taller, all of it above the baseline, so the whole box's text sits 0.42-0.54 pt below PowerPoint's and the slide's SSIM fell 0.9807 -> 0.9494 with the I-006 fix.

## What PowerPoint does

Each run on a line takes 1.2 x its own size, split above and below the baseline at its font's win ascent : win descent (I-016). The a:br that ends the line adds its own font at the size of the line's last run, and so does the paragraph mark on the last line when the paragraph has an a:endParaRPr (even one that names no font; with no endParaRPr the mark does not count). The a:br's and endParaRPr's own sizes do not count, and a bullet's font does not count at all. The line takes the largest part above the baseline and the largest part below it and scales both down together to 1.2 x its largest run size. So 18 pt Calibri (16.85 above, 4.75 below) with an Arial run (17.50, 4.10) is 17.50 + 4.75 = 22.25 scaled to 21.6: the baseline 16.99 pt below the line top (PowerPoint 17.05 on its 0.12 pt grid), and the line is no taller than an all-Calibri line. With Courier New (15.87, 5.73) the baseline rises to 16.12 (PowerPoint 16.14). A smaller run in another font counts at its own size: 24 pt Calibri with a 22 pt Courier New run gives 21.97 (PowerPoint 21.97); with a 20 pt run nothing changes. Line spacing other than 100% (I-017) then works on these parts. The 13.5 pt Calibri line with an Arial Hebrew run on slide 674: 12.74 instead of Calibri's 12.64, so 0.1 pt lower, with the line height unchanged.

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: CTextMetrics kept the largest ascent, descent and line gap of the line's fonts separately and added them up, so a mixed line grew. 18 pt Calibri with an Arial run had its first baseline at 17.62 pt and the line 0.77 pt taller than PowerPoint's; with Courier New 0.97 pt taller. The fonts of the a:br and the paragraph mark were ignored on lines with text.

## Suspected cause

Confirmed: Euro-Office combined the fonts on a line by Word's rule (the largest ascent, descent and line gap added up) instead of PowerPoint's (the largest parts of each run's 1.2 em line, scaled back to 1.2 x the largest size), and left out the font of the a:br or paragraph mark that ends the line.

## Where to look

word/Editor/Paragraph_Recalculate.js: Paragraph.private_ApplySlideLineFonts, called from private_RecalculateLineMetrics, with CParagraphRecalculateStateWrap.LineTextHeight, LineLastTextHeight and LineBreakTextPr; word/Editor/Run.js: ParaRun.Recalculate_LineMetrics records them.

## Fix idea

Measure first: a test deck with lines of Calibri plus one run in another font (Arial, Times New Roman, Segoe UI, Cambria, a larger Calibri run) at 100%, 90% and 150% line spacing and two sizes, with the baselines read from PowerPoint's PDF. Then change, for slide text only, how the line's ascent, descent and gap are combined. Related to I-016 (Times New Roman first baseline) and I-017 (first baseline at other line spacings).
