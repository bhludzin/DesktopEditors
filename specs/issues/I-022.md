# I-022: No line break after an en dash, so '8:34–35' is moved to the next line as one unit

## What differs

Found while checking the four slides that still broke lines differently after K2, not by the review (the slide's finding, S0116-1, is grouped under K2). Slide 116: in a 13.5 pt Calibri quote PowerPoint ends a line with 'and I know your doing” (Morm. 8:34–' and starts the next with '35)'. Euro-Office has no break opportunity after the en dash, moves '8:34–35)' to the next line as one unit, and the rest of the box reflows: 16 of 22 lines matched.

## What PowerPoint does

A line may break after an en dash (U+2013), between digits ('8:34–35', '1850–1851', 'pp. 12–14') as between letters ('slain–army'). The dash stays at the end of the line. Measured on test deck dash-super-rules slide 1, 8 boxes from 96 to 222 pt wide. Em dash, hyphen-minus and slash already matched Euro-Office (slides 2-4).

## What Euro-Office does

A break is allowed after a character whose IsSpaceAfter() is true; CRunText.private_IsSpaceAfter sets it for the hyphen-minus and the em dash but not for the en dash.

## Suspected cause

The en dash is missing from the break-after characters used for slide text.

## Where to look

word/Editor/Run.js: ParaRun.prototype.Recalculate_Range (isBreakAfter); word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Text.js: CRunText.prototype.private_IsSpaceAfter.

## Fix idea

For slide text, treat U+2013 as a break-after character in Recalculate_Range.
