## What differs

Found while fixing K5 and narrowed by I-027. K5 puts lines where PowerPoint draws them vertically; I-027 starts each line where PowerPoint does (on the PDF's 1/3000-inch grid, stretched about the slide's left edge by the 0.116 pt over 720 pt and 0.125 pt over 960 pt this issue first measured on the textscale decks) and draws PowerPoint's long glyph runs. What is left is along the line: on the 1646-slide deck (run i027-h, lines of at least 300 pt paired by text), Euro-Office's line length minus PowerPoint's, median per mille of the length by PowerPoint's Tf (share within 0.5): Calibri 13.56 +0.25 (88%), 18 +0.41 (69%), 17.04 +0.55, 20.04 +0.73, 24.96 +0.76, 15.96 +0.66, 12.72 +1.09 (6%), 10.08 +0.90, 13.08 +0.92, 10.56 -0.40, 27.96 -0.75; Calibri Bold 9 +1.02 (1%), 12 +1.17, 13.56 -0.24, 13.58 -0.62, 24 +0.04, 30 +0.15, 39.96 +0.27; Arial 10.56 -0.15 (99%). On a 600 pt line 1 per mille is 0.6 pt, about 0.7 px at the review's 800 px; 596 of the deck's 16765 long lines still differ by more than 0.5 pt and 30 by more than 1 pt.

## What PowerPoint does

On top of I-027's extra cells, draws glyph runs shorter than K2's layout (at a few sizes longer) by a fraction that depends on font and size (above). Within a line the glyphs are closer together by about that fraction of their distance from the line's start (on the 838-slide deck, mostly 13.5 pt, about 0.00019: 0.02 pt over 100 pt).

## What Euro-Office does

Draws each glyph run at its layout length plus I-027's extra cells, so its lines are longer than PowerPoint's by the fractions above.

## Suspected cause

Partly found: how PowerPoint places glyphs on its 600-dpi device and how its PDF writer encodes those places, not a scale. Glyphs inside a word advance by whole 600-dpi pixels; on lines of words each word start is put on the whole pixel nearest its layout position (measured from the line start); on one-glyph lines the glyphs are pulled back to the layout by one pixel when they have drifted about 3.5 px ahead or about 5 px behind. The PDF writer draws glyphs at their PDF widths and writes the difference as whole 1/1000 em only now and then, rounding it and not making up the remainder, so the PDF falls behind those places along the line (at 17 pt about 0.7 cell per word). The writer's exact rule is not known; see the log.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph/draw/content-draw-state.js: ParagraphContentDrawState.prototype.getSlideGlyphX (I-027), where each glyph's drawn position on a slide is decided.

## Fix idea

Parked (2026-11-23): what is left is 0.15 to 0.7 pt on a 600 pt line (under 1 px at the harness's 800 px slide width). To go on, the device places and the writer's rule need to be told apart: a test deck of lines whose device places are certain (one-glyph words separated by spaces, two-glyph words, words of two alternating glyphs) at the sizes above, then a writer rule that reproduces every TJ on it, then simulate both in getSlideGlyphX (I-027's drawing code) and check widthsum.py on both decks.
