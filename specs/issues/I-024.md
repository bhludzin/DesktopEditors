# I-024: Text is drawn about 0.02 pt per 100 pt wider along a line than PowerPoint draws it, and PowerPoint's PDF stretches line starts by about 0.12 pt over the slide's width

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | trivial |
| Slides | 0 |
| Findings | 0 |
| Categories | text-position, text-glyph-spacing |
| Confidence in the cause | low |

## What differs

Found while fixing K5, not by the review: K5 puts lines where PowerPoint draws them vertically, and the same line pairs show what is left horizontally. On the 838-slide deck (15446 lines paired by text) PowerPoint's line starts are on average 0.025 pt right of Euro-Office's (sd 0.05 pt). On the textscale test decks (one-line, left-aligned boxes with zero insets over the whole slide) PowerPoint's line starts are stretched about the slide's left edge by 0.116 pt over a 720 pt width, 0.125 over 960, 0.112 over 540 and 0.128 over 1440, like the vertical 0.1075 pt stretch K5 copies. Within a line PowerPoint's glyphs are closer together than Euro-Office's by about 0.00019 x their distance from the line start (0.02 pt over 100 pt), which K2's 1/576-inch layout does not account for. At the review's resolution all of this is under 0.1 px.

## What PowerPoint does

Draws a line's start about (0.12 pt / slide width) x its distance from the slide's left edge further right than its layout position, and the glyphs in the line about 0.02% closer together than the 1/576-inch layout (K2) puts them. The glyphs are drawn at 600-dpi rounded advances (see K2).

## What Euro-Office does

Draws each line from its layout start and its glyphs at their layout advances, with no stretch horizontally (K5 stretches vertically).

## Suspected cause

PowerPoint's PDF export scales text positions slightly about the slide's top-left corner, horizontally as vertically, and draws glyphs at advances a little narrower than its layout's.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph.js: GetSlideLinePos (K5) can move a line's start; the glyphs within a line are placed by ParaRun.prototype.Draw_Elements (word/Editor/Run.js).

## Fix idea

Fit the horizontal stretch per slide width on the textscale decks and the compression within lines per font and size on the deck before changing anything. Then stretch x in GetSlideLinePos as y is stretched, and scale glyph offsets within the line if the compression turns out to be one factor.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
