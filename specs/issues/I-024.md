## What differs

Found while fixing K5 and narrowed by I-027. K5 puts lines where PowerPoint draws them vertically; I-027 starts each line where PowerPoint does (on the PDF's 1/3000-inch grid, stretched about the slide's left edge by the 0.116 pt over 720 pt and 0.125 pt over 960 pt this issue first measured on the textscale decks) and draws PowerPoint's long glyph runs. What is left is along the line: on the 1646-slide deck (run i027-h, lines of at least 300 pt paired by text), Euro-Office's line length minus PowerPoint's, median per mille of the length by PowerPoint's Tf (share within 0.5): Calibri 13.56 +0.25 (88%), 18 +0.41 (69%), 17.04 +0.55, 20.04 +0.73, 24.96 +0.76, 15.96 +0.66, 12.72 +1.09 (6%), 10.08 +0.90, 13.08 +0.92, 10.56 -0.40, 27.96 -0.75; Calibri Bold 9 +1.02 (1%), 12 +1.17, 13.56 -0.24, 13.58 -0.62, 24 +0.04, 30 +0.15, 39.96 +0.27; Arial 10.56 -0.15 (99%). On a 600 pt line 1 per mille is 0.6 pt, about 0.7 px at the review's 800 px; 596 of the deck's 16765 long lines still differ by more than 0.5 pt and 30 by more than 1 pt.

## What PowerPoint does

Lays text out with K2's model and draws it in I-027's glyph runs, but places each glyph on its 600-dpi device and has its PDF writer encode those places. Device: whole pixels at ppem = round(size x 600 / 72) pixels per em (13.5 pt 113, 18 pt 150). A glyph after a space (a word start) goes to the pixel nearest its layout position measured from the glyph run's start (an exact half goes down below 1024 px and up from there). Any other glyph follows the one before by that glyph's advance in whole pixels (round(advance x ppem)) plus its kerning in whole pixels, moved back a pixel at a time while it is more than 17.5 cells (3.5 px) ahead of its layout position, and on a pixel at a time while its end (its start plus its own pixel advance) is 27.5 cells (5.5 px) or more short of the next glyph's layout position. PDF writer: draws each glyph by its PDF width W (1/1000 em: the advance x 1000 rounded to 1/64 and then to a whole unit, so Calibri i and l are 230 for 229.49) at the PDF font size Tf: 5 x ppem cells stretched down the slide from the baseline as the baseline is (K5), round((y + 5 ppem)(1 + s)) - round(y (1 + s)) cells for a baseline y cells down before the stretch, so 13.5 pt is 13.56 and in about one line in eight 13.584. It adds up (device advance, with I-027's extra cells, minus W x Tf / 1000) in units of Tf / 1000 since its last adjustment; when the sum reaches 3 units either way it writes an adjustment of the sum less a half, cut towards zero, before the next glyph and starts again from zero. The remainder it drops is the shortfall: 0.27 per mille at 13.5 pt, 0.5 at 18 pt, 1.3 at 12.75 pt. Spaces before a run's first other glyph are not in the PDF's text: that glyph starts it where the device puts it. On lines of one glyph repeated the PDF also uses a Tc.

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: drew each glyph run at its layout length plus I-027's extra cells, so its lines were longer than PowerPoint's (median per mille on the 1646-slide deck, run i027-h: Calibri 13.56 +0.25, 18 +0.41, 12.72 +1.09, Calibri Bold 9 +1.02). Now, wherever I-027 draws glyph runs (720 x 540 and 960 x 540 pt slides, text not turned, left to right), draws each glyph where PowerPoint's device and PDF writer put it, from the advances the shaper measured; runs with character spacing and glyphs whose advance is not known (ligatures, fonts emboldened in code) stay at their layout widths. Run i024-c: the medians are within 0.2 per mille at most sizes (Calibri 13.56 -0.05, 18 -0.03, 12.72 -0.17; Calibri Bold 9 -0.39), glyphs within 0.03 pt of PowerPoint's 20% -> 84% (slides 100 to 400; median 0.087 -> 0.006 pt), and of the lines at least 300 pt long those more than 0.3 pt off in width 3012 -> 568 and more than 0.5 pt 596 -> 326. Line breaks are unchanged: this is drawing only.

## Suspected cause

Cause found: PowerPoint places glyphs on its 600-dpi device in whole pixels and its PDF writer encodes those places from the glyphs' PDF widths, with adjustments written only once they reach 3 units and the remainder dropped (see powerpoint). K2's layout and I-027's glyph runs hold; only the drawing differed.

## Open questions

- About 4% of word starts land a pixel from the one nearest their layout position (13.5 pt, slides 1 to 150: 61 of 1631). The misses lie within 0.08 px of a half pixel and follow no rule tried: a scale and offset, the line's start on the slide, layout advances or kerning in 1/64 px or other units.
- At 18 pt the writer's sum often comes to exactly 3 units (a cell is 4/3 unit at Tf 18). PowerPoint writes about half of the +3 cases and most of the -3 ones, apparently by float error; the model writes them all. Lines whose adjustments are reproduced exactly: 13.5 pt 63%, 18 pt 32%, 12.75 pt 95%; the line ends agree to within 0.06 per mille.
- Glyph runs are measured from their own start. For a run that starts inside a line (a new a:r) PowerPoint may measure word starts from the line's start.
- Lines of one glyph repeated get a Tc from PowerPoint, which is not followed: the glyph deck's lines of n, i, m and o stay up to 0.67 pt off.
- The PDF font size rule fits 178 of the glyph deck's 180 lines with a stretch of 0.106 pt over the slide's height, and 173 with K5's 0.1075 pt, which is used.
- Lines that were already shorter than PowerPoint's for other reasons come out 0.3 to 0.5 per mille shorter still, so lines more than 1 pt off went 30 -> 52 (slide 583 Calibri Bold 15.96 pt -1.03 -> -1.47 pt; slides 711 and 1156 Calibri 18 pt about -0.97 -> -1.34). Calibri Bold 9 pt is now 0.39 per mille short and Calibri Bold 13.56 0.16; Calibri 27.96 stays 0.75 short.
- Glyph sizes are not changed: Euro-Office's PDF draws the run size (13.5 pt) where PowerPoint's draws 13.56 (K2).
