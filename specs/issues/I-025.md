# I-025: PowerPoint downsamples pictures drawn above about 270 dpi to 200 dpi in its PDF; Euro-Office embeds them at full size

| | |
|---|---|

| Severity | trivial |
| Slides | 0 |
| Findings | 0 |
| Categories | picture |
| Confidence in the cause | medium |

## What differs

Found while fixing K6, not by the review. 10 of the 838-slide deck's 70 media are downsampled in PowerPoint's PDF (99 uses on 22 pages): image53 (451 x 542, 31 uses at 610 dpi) is a 147 x 177 image, image24 (70 x 184, 48 uses at 285 dpi) 49 x 129, image56 (418 x 623 at 975 dpi) 85 x 127. Euro-Office embeds every picture at its full size, so these pictures are sharper than PowerPoint's when the PDF is zoomed in. At the review's 800 px the renderer scales them down to fewer pixels than either image has, so the difference hardly shows there.

## What PowerPoint does

A medium whose largest use is drawn at more than about 270 dpi (kept at 261, downsampled from 285) is stored at floor(w x 200 / dpi) x floor(h x 200 / dpi), the dpi of that largest use, so 200 dpi there: picture-rules' 1200 x 800 picture, drawn at 720 and 360 dpi, is one 666 x 444 image for both. image60, drawn at 199 to 416 dpi, is kept whole. The resampling filter is not known.

## What Euro-Office does

Embeds every picture at its full size (K6 encodes it as PowerPoint does, but does not resample it).

## Suspected cause

PowerPoint's PDF export applies a 200-dpi image budget per medium at its largest use; core's PDF writer has no such step.

## Where to look

core PdfFile/PdfWriter.cpp: UpdateSlidePictureBrush and LoadSlideImage (K6). The decision needs every use of a medium, so the image would have to be written (or rewritten) once the document is done, for instance in CPdfWriter's save.

## Fix idea

Record each slide image's source and the largest size it is drawn at (from the CTM) as pages are written; before saving, re-encode the images whose largest use is above the threshold at 200 dpi there. Pin the threshold (between 261 and 285 dpi) and the filter on a test deck first.

## Open questions

- image64 (1024 x 768, cropped, drawn at about 217 dpi) is downsampled to 274 x 206 in PowerPoint's PDF, below the threshold the other media show; the crop may count differently.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
