# I-026: A tiled picture fill with flip is mirrored in Euro-Office's PDF; PowerPoint's PDF tiles it unmirrored

## What differs

Found while fixing K6 on the picture-rules test deck, not by the review; neither deck tiles a picture. Slide 3 fills a 400 x 260 pt rectangle with a 320 x 200 picture tiled at 100% with flip="xy": Euro-Office's PDF mirrors every other tile across and down, PowerPoint's repeats the same tile (SSIM 0.938 for the slide).

## What PowerPoint does

Its PDF draws the tiled fill as a tiling pattern of one unmirrored tile (BBox 0 0 320 200, XStep 320, YStep 200, scaled 0.75 to 240 x 150 pt, the picture at 96 dpi), whatever the flip.

## What Euro-Office does

Its PDF draws a tiling pattern of 2 x 2 tiles (480 x 300 pt) with the picture mirrored in the second column and row (core's imagetilepatterntype_InverseXY).

## Suspected cause

PowerPoint's PDF export ignores a:tile/@flip. Not checked: whether PowerPoint mirrors the tiles on screen, as the format says it should.

## Where to look

core PdfFile/PdfWriter.cpp: UpdateBrush (the tile modes) and SrcWriter/Pattern.cpp: CImageTilePattern.

## Fix idea

First check PowerPoint's own view (a slide exported as an image) to tell an export quirk from the rule; if its view also ignores the flip, tile without mirroring for slides.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
