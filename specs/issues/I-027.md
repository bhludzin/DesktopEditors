## What differs

Found by the slide-by-slide comparison of the 1646-slide deck (a later version of the 838-slide deck: 377 slides the same, 75 with changed text, 1194 new), run k6-c. Every one of the 28138 lines paired by text between the two PDFs sits on PowerPoint's baseline (all within 0.124 pt, 28121 within 0.03 pt) and starts where PowerPoint's starts (within 0.33 pt), but the lengths differ: of the 16766 paired lines at least 300 pt long, 1238 differ by more than 0.5 pt and 312 by more than 1 pt (308 of them shorter in Euro-Office). At the review's 800 px a 1 to 1.6 pt drift moves the second half of a line by 1 to 2 px, and this is the main difference left on the deck: the 93 slides with a line more than 1 pt off average SSIM 0.958 (15 0.815, 39 0.829, 1283 0.855, 35 0.855, 38 0.863, 1371 0.901, 1487 0.906, 551 0.913, 1590 0.914), and the 525 slides whose lines all match within 0.3 pt average 0.997. Of the 120 slides under 0.98, 96 have a line more than 0.6 pt off and the rest 0.4 to 0.6 pt. The deck's three line breaks that differ on slides 15, 906 and 1308 turned out not to be part of this (see I-028). The 838-slide deck is mostly 13.5 pt text, where PowerPoint keeps to the layout on 82% of long lines, so its review saw only the edge of this (slide 255's 12.75 pt text about 0.1% long, slide 93's 'like').

## What PowerPoint does

Lays text out with K2's model (its line breaks follow the model), but draws it on the PDF's 1/3000-inch grid (one cell, 0.024 pt) in runs of glyphs, and some runs come out longer than the layout. A layout position x (in cells from the slide's left edge) is drawn at P = x x (1 + a) + b, with a = 1.48323e-4 and b = 0.0304 cells on 720 x 540 pt slides and a = 1.28602e-4 and b = 0.0311 on 960 x 540 (fitted on boxes moved in 1-EMU steps; this is the 0.12 pt stretch of line starts I-024 first measured). A glyph run starts at P rounded to the nearest cell, which leaves a phase s, the sixteenths of a cell P lies past that cell (floor(16 P) - 16 x the cell, -8 to 7). Each glyph of the run then advances its layout width plus floor((s + 8 + round(16 x n x a)) / 16) cells, n being the glyph's layout width in cells: at s = 7 every glyph at least about 5.06 pt wide adds one cell to everything after it, at s = 6 every glyph at least about 15 pt wide, and at lower phases none at text sizes. The phase is the run's own and is not carried from glyph to glyph (only leading spaces at a line's start carry it into the first glyph). A 60-glyph run at s = 7 is thus about 1.4 pt longer than its layout, and the next line of the same paragraph, starting at another phase, may not be: this is the variation between lines of one run (slide 1283). A glyph run restarts at each a:r (except that an a:r of only spaces continues into the next), before and after a hyphen, en dash or em dash (the deck draws every hyphen alone), at tabs, pictures and equations, and at each line; spaces in the middle of a line that open a run restart it at its first visible glyph. The extra cells are only drawn on left, justified and distributed lines, and not in a run with character spacing (PowerPoint draws those with Tc), nor in the run after a hyphen or dash, after a space in the middle of a line, or after leading spaces in the middle of a line. Left and justified lines start at their indent rounded to the nearest 1/600 inch, and the text after a bullet starts at its own rounded marL.

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: drew every glyph at its layout position (K2), so each line came out at its layout length; on the 1646-slide deck 1237 of 16765 lines at least 300 pt long differed from PowerPoint's in length by more than 0.5 pt and 311 by more than 1 pt (median, Euro-Office minus PowerPoint, per mille of the length, by PowerPoint's Tf: Calibri 17.04 -0.51, 20.04 -1.41, 24.96 -1.50, 26.04 -2.32, 32.04 -1.29). Now, for slides of 720 x 540 or 960 x 540 pt with text that is not rotated or skewed and runs left to right, draws as PowerPoint's PDF does: each glyph run starts on the stretched grid with its phase, its glyphs get PowerPoint's extra cells, and left and justified indents are rounded to 1/600 inch. Line breaks are unchanged: this is drawing only. After the fix 596 of those lines differ by more than 0.5 pt and 30 by more than 1 pt (17.04 +0.55, 20.04 +0.73, 24.96 +0.76, 26.04 -0.05, 32.04 +0.42; what is left is I-024).

## Suspected cause

Cause found: PowerPoint's PDF export positions glyphs on its 1/3000-inch grid run by run, from a start that is its layout position stretched about the slide's left edge, and a run whose start lies 7/16 of a cell (or 6/16, for wide glyphs) past the cell it rounds to gains a cell per glyph from the stretch. It looks like a fixed-point step in sixteenths of a cell that adds each glyph's stretch to the start's fraction instead of the running one (a guess). K2's model holds for the layout and the line breaks; Euro-Office's line lengths differed only in drawing.

## Fix idea

Done (see euro_office). Left: other slide sizes (fit a and b on a deck like linelength-cal720 for each width), and the open questions below.

## Open questions

- Runs drawn in a Type0 font (the deck's curly quotes) gain extra cells at s = 7 too, but fit best with about 0.75 x the threshold width; the rule's 1.0 x is kept, and slides 1483, 434 and 278 are slightly worse for it (1483 SSIM -0.006).
- Glyph runs that start in the middle of a line after another glyph (a new a:r) get the extra cells: 523 of the deck's 572 such runs match PowerPoint, 49 do not (slide 1092).
- Centred and right-aligned lines get no extra cells, since their starts are not known to a sixteenth of a cell; 'Heavenly' on slides 995 to 1013 (centred, Calibri Bold 13.5 pt) now starts 0.31 pt right of PowerPoint's, 0.22 before.
- At 18 pt the deck's lines gain 0.77 times the extra cells the rule predicts.
- A box whose edge lies exactly half a cell from the grid is borderline: PowerPoint's measured edges scatter by 1 EMU.
- Only 720 x 540 and 960 x 540 pt slides are handled; other sizes are drawn at the layout as before.
- PowerPoint writes the font size on a 600-dpi step (17 -> Tf 17.04, 20 -> 20.04, 13.5 -> 13.56, 12.75 -> 12.72, on some lines one 0.024 pt step higher) and Euro-Office the run size; not changed.
- Lines that mix runs of different fonts or weights (slides 776 and 1112) are not paired by linecmp.py and are left out of the counts.
- 15 pt and 13 pt Calibri lines depart from K2's model by about +3 per mille in both PDFs alike, so the model misses something on those slides (character spacing?); not counted as differences here.
