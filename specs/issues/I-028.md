# I-028: Line breaks within 0.005 pt of the edge go both ways: PowerPoint wraps some lines that fit by K2 and keeps some that are over

## What differs

Found by the 1646-slide deck's comparison (run k6-c). With K2's widths computed exactly (I-031), PowerPoint's line breaks follow the edge test everywhere except within 0.005 pt of the edge, where they go both ways. Wrapped though they fit: slide 15 of the 1646-slide deck (Calibri 18 pt, 'again.', by 0.0029 pt; SSIM 0.910 before I-024, 0.938 after), 1308 (13.5 pt, 'I', 0.0022) and 3185 of the 3963-slide deck (18 pt, 'Russian', 0.0049). Kept though over: 806 of the 838-slide deck and 1564 of the 1646-slide deck (the same line, 13.5 pt, by 0.0024 pt) and 962 of the 3963-slide deck (18 pt, 0.0029). Wrapped at only 0.0012 pt over: 93 and 906 (one line). The other slides the first comparison put here (906 and 93) were I-031. Slides 279, 280 and 349 of the 1646-slide deck are text extraction only: PowerPoint's PDF maps the Hebrew marks to text in another order, and the glyphs are drawn the same.

## What PowerPoint does

Within 0.005 pt of the edge, wraps or keeps a word in a way no rule tried explains (see the log). Outside that band it follows K2's widths computed exactly.

## What Euro-Office does

Follows K2's widths exactly (I-031), so it gets these 6 slides wrong, 3 each way.

## Suspected cause

PowerPoint's own arithmetic: some rounding or accumulation in its line measure below 0.005 pt that is not K2's.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph/TextShaper.js: CParagraphTextShaper.prototype.private_GetLayoutWidth (K2's widths); word/Editor/Run.js: ParaRun.prototype.Recalculate_Range (line breaking).

## Fix idea

Collect more decisions within 0.01 pt of the edge (a test deck: one line made to end 0 to 0.01 pt past the edge in 1-EMU steps of the box width, with and without kerning pairs) and look for the unit PowerPoint measures in. Low value: about 1 slide in 1000.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
