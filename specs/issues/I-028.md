# I-028: Three line breaks differ on the 1646-slide deck: Euro-Office keeps a word on a line that fits by 0.04 to 0.14 pt, PowerPoint wraps it

## What differs

Found by the 1646-slide deck's comparison (run k6-c, unchanged by I-027 in run i027-h). On slides 15 (Calibri 18 pt, 'again.'), 906 (13.5 pt, 'like') and 1308 (13.5 pt, 'I') Euro-Office keeps the word on a line that ends 0.14, 0.06 and 0.04 pt inside the text area, and PowerPoint moves it to the next line, so the lines below differ too (slide 15 SSIM 0.910, the deck's lowest; 906 0.985; 1308 0.978). Slide 93 of the 838-slide deck ('like', a word within 0.01 pt of the edge, SSIM 0.981) looks like the same case. The 1646-slide deck's other lines that break differently (slides 279, 280 and 349) are text extraction only: PowerPoint's PDF maps the Hebrew marks to text in another order, and the glyphs are drawn the same.

## What PowerPoint does

Moves the word to the next line, although by K2's model the line fits with 0.04 to 0.14 pt to spare.

## What Euro-Office does

Keeps the word on the line: by K2's model, which Euro-Office lays out with, it fits.

## Suspected cause

Not known. PowerPoint's line breaking may measure the line a little wider than K2's model (another rounding of the advances or of the kerning, the trailing space or punctuation counted), or keep a small margin. I-027's extra cells are drawing only and do not explain it: the deck's other long lines break as K2's model does.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph/TextShaper.js: CParagraphTextShaper.prototype.private_GetLayoutWidth (K2's widths); word/Editor/Run.js: ParaRun.prototype.Recalculate_Range (line breaking).

## Fix idea

Measure each case first (the text area's width, the line's width by K2 with and without the trailing space and kerning). Then build a test deck with one line made to fit by 0 to 0.3 pt in 1-EMU steps of the box width, at 13.5 and 18 pt, to find the margin PowerPoint breaks at.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
