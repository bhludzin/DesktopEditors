## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). A knife emoji (U+1F52A) at the end of a 14-pt line is drawn by Euro-Office as the black outline glyph of Segoe UI Emoji. PowerPoint writes it as a 96x81 colour bitmap (brown handle, grey blade). The position and size match; the colour and shape do not. Slides (11): 734-736, 751-758.

## What PowerPoint does

Colour emoji bitmap placed in the text line.

## What Euro-Office does

Monochrome Segoe UI Emoji outline glyph in the text colour.

## Suspected cause

The native text renderer and PDF writer draw only the outline (glyf) layer of a COLR/CPAL colour font; the colour layers are ignored.

## Where to look

DesktopEditors/core/DesktopEditor/fontengine (glyph rendering) and core/PdfFile/PdfWriter.cpp (text output)

## Fix idea

For glyphs from a COLR font, draw each colour layer as a filled glyph in its CPAL colour, or rasterise the emoji to an image the way PowerPoint does.

## Open questions

- Small (one glyph per slide), but the colour is wrong, so it is ranked major. The native renderer path for colour fonts was not traced.
