## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). On slides 471 and 2822 a line with its next word is wider than the text area by K2's model (471: 693.50391 pt against 693.5; 2822: 687.50488 against 687.5), and PowerPoint wraps the word, so every later line of the box reflows (471: 2 lines differ, SSIM 0.975; 2822: 3 lines, 0.949). Release 9.3.8 wrapped both, with its own widths. The same cause kept two of I-028's lines (slide 93 of the 838-slide deck and 906 of the 1646-slide deck, over by 0.0012 pt).

## What PowerPoint does

Wraps a word when the line with it is wider than the text area (box width minus insets, rounded down to whole 1/8 pt, K3) by K2's widths: advances rounded to 1/8 pt and kerning unrounded, computed exactly. On 99144 of PowerPoint's break decisions in the three decks (lines kept, and lines with the next word wrapped, in paragraphs of one Calibri face and size) this is wrong 64 times, and only 6 of those lie within 0.05 pt of the edge: 5 within 0.003 pt of it (I-028) and slide 1020 of the 3963-slide deck (a hyphenated word, 0.046 pt).

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: kept each run item's width cut down to a whole 1/16384 mm (TEXTWIDTH_DIVIDER), about 0.0001 pt lost a glyph, so a line of 100 glyphs came out up to 0.01 pt shorter than its K2 width and lines over the edge by less than that were kept. Now slide text keeps its widths rounded to the nearest unit, so lines are measured as K2 has them, to within 0.0005 pt. I-024's word starts, which corrected for the cut by half a unit a glyph, now take the layout position as it is, and its test for a word start exactly half a pixel from two pixels (1 word start in 24 is one) allows 0.01 px instead of 0.001, since the item widths put a position a few thousandths of a pixel off.

## Suspected cause

Cause found: the run items' widths were truncated to 1/16384 mm, which for slide text laid out to K2's whole 1/8 pt made lines slightly short of the edge test.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Base.js: AscWord.ToTextWidth (rounds in slides), used by CRunText.SetWidth, SetTemporaryWidth and SetWidthVisible (Text.js) and CRunSpace.SetWidth (Space.js); word/Editor/Paragraph/draw/content-draw-state.js getSlideDeviceX (I-024's layout position and its half-pixel test).

## Fix idea

Done (see euro_office).
