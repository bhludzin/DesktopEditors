## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Pictures with spPr scene3d camera orthographicFront and rot lon=10800000 (180 degrees about the vertical axis, 931 has 10799977) are shown mirrored left-right by PowerPoint. Euro-Office draws them unmirrored. 931 SSIM 0.747; 1046 0.929 (also N-02). Slides (2): 931, 1046.

## What PowerPoint does

Draws a flat object (no sp3d) with a scene3d camera as the camera sees it, picture, geometry, outline and text alike. In the object's own frame, about its centre and after its flips (y down), the camera maps (u, v) to x = u cos(lon), y = u sin(lon) sin(lat) + v cos(lat), then turns the result counter-clockwise by rev; the object's rotation applies after that. So lon 180 mirrors it left to right and lat 180 top to bottom (text reads mirrored), both together or rev 180 turn it half round, and other angles foreshorten it; seen edge-on (lon or lat 90) it is not drawn. perspectiveFront gives the same result at lon 180, and at lon 60 its quadrants land in the same places. The PDF holds the result as an image rasterised at about 201.6 dpi with a soft mask, about 0.42 pt larger than the object. With lightRig threePt from the top, a square-on face is lit: the front face (lon and lat 0 or both 180) maps each colour channel v to round(1.03894 v + 5.007), the back face (exactly one of them 180) to round(0.77354 v + 9.984), both clipped to 0-255, on pictures and fills alike (4472C4 becomes (76, 123, 209) in front and (63, 98, 162) behind; 808080 becomes 138). A tilted face keeps the slope and gets a larger offset (lat 60: about 29; lat 300: about 44; lon 60: about 1). Measured on the test deck scene3d-rules (86 cases: camera angles, flips and rotations, an arrow, outlines, text, perspectiveFront, and lossless grey steps for the light).

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: scene3d was skipped when reading, so the object was drawn as stored. Now, in slides, scene3d is read into the shape's properties, and a shape, picture or connector whose camera is orthographicFront or perspectiveFront is drawn through the camera's map, inserted between its flips and its rotation: everything it draws (fill, outline, text, picture) moves with it, and nothing is drawn edge-on. A picture fill on a square-on face under threePt from the top gets PowerPoint's light, baked in after the picture's colour effects (I-030). On the test deck every picture case puts each quadrant where PowerPoint does, the shape and text cases match, and the square-on light fits PowerPoint's to the level (front 1.0390 v + 4.99, back 0.7735 v + 9.99).

## Suspected cause

Cause found: the PPTY spPr reader skipped record 5 (scene3d), so the camera never reached the shape model (only theme effect styles and SmartArt data read scene3d).

## Where to look

sdkjs common/Shapes/Serialize.js ReadSpPr (scene3d read into CSpPr.scene3d as an AscFormat.Scene3d, outside the history; common/Drawings/Format/Format.js CSpPr copies it). common/Drawings/Format/GraphicObjectBase.js: drawWithSlideCamera (getSlideScene3dCamera, getSlideCameraMap) moves the object's transforms by base^-1 L base for one draw, L being the camera's map through the flips about the centre; getSlideSceneLighting adds the light to the picture's colour effects. Called at the top of CShape.draw (Shape.js) and CImageShape.draw (Image.js); word/Drawing/ShapeDrawer.js getBlipEffectsImage also takes a picture fill with scene3d. Presentation editor only.

## Fix idea

Done (see euro_office). Not done: the light on fills, outlines and text (I-038 has the rule), on tilted faces, and for other rigs or a rotated rig; other camera presets (oblique, isometric, perspective other than front); sp3d (bevels, extrusion); the camera on a group; PowerPoint's rasterising of the result (the PDF keeps vectors and text). scene3d is not written back when saving.

## Open questions

- perspectiveFront is drawn as orthographic; PowerPoint's perspective was only compared at lon 180 (the same) and lon 60 (the quadrants land where the orthographic map puts them; the edges were not measured).
- Hit testing and text editing still use the object's own frame, so a mirrored text box is edited unmirrored.
