## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Pictures with spPr scene3d camera orthographicFront and rot lon=10800000 (180 degrees about the vertical axis, 931 has 10799977) are shown mirrored left-right by PowerPoint. Euro-Office draws them unmirrored. 931 SSIM 0.747; 1046 0.929 (also N-02). Slides (2): 931, 1046.

## What PowerPoint does

Picture mirrored horizontally.

## What Euro-Office does

Picture drawn as stored.

## Suspected cause

spPr scene3d/sp3d is not carried into the presentation shape model (only theme effect styles and SmartArt data hold scene3d), so the camera rotation is never applied.

## Where to look

sdkjs/common/Shapes/Serialize.js (spPr reader: no scene3d); sdkjs/common/Drawings/Format/Format.js CSpPr; x2t PPTX-to-binary conversion

## Fix idea

Read scene3d camera rot; for orthographic cameras with lon near 180 degrees (and lat 0) apply a horizontal flip, and for lat near 180 a vertical flip; other angles could map to an affine foreshortening.

- Only the 180-degree case occurs in this deck.
