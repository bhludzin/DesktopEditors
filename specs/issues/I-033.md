# I-033: An opening quote followed by spaces cannot end a line

## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). A paragraph has a left double quote (U+201C) directly before spaces. PowerPoint ends a line with it (the line ends "slavery.“"). Euro-Office treats U+201C as CANT_BE_AT_END and glues the following space to it, so the quote moves to the next line and all later lines reflow (PowerPoint 21 lines, Euro-Office 22; SSIM 0.755). Slides (1): 154.

## What PowerPoint does

Breaks at the spaces after an opening mark like at any other space, so the mark can end a line: measured for the left quotes (U+201C, U+2018), ( [ { $ GBP and Yen signs, and the East Asian and full-width brackets and signs Word keeps off a line's end (U+3008, U+300C, U+300E, U+3010, U+301D, U+FF08, U+FF04), alone or after a word ('slavery.' + U+201C), followed by one or two spaces. A mark attached to the next word ('(camp', U+201C + 'army', '$100') stays with it, as before. Measured on the test deck openpunct-rules (48 wrapped boxes of 13.5 pt Calibri, kern 1200; slide 5 holds the attached controls).

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: a space after such a mark was added to the word (Run.js, para_Space), so the mark and the spaces moved to the next line with the following word. Now, in slides, the space is a break opportunity after any such mark; a non-breaking space before the space keeps the old rule (not measured). On the test deck all 48 boxes break like PowerPoint (15 before; slide 5's controls were already right).

## Suspected cause

Cause found: ParaRun.Recalculate_Range glued a para_Space to the word when the item before it could not end a line (g_aPunctuation CANT_BE_AT_END: Word's rule).

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Run.js ParaRun.Recalculate_Range, case para_Space (slides: Para.IsSlideText()).

## Fix idea

Done (see euro_office). Word documents keep the old rule.

## Open questions

- Measured in Latin text only; a space after an opening bracket inside Japanese or Chinese text was not tested.
- A non-breaking space followed by a space still glues (not measured).
