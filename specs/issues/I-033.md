# I-033: An opening quote followed by spaces cannot end a line

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | open |
| Severity | major |
| Slides | 0 |

## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). A paragraph has a left double quote (U+201C) directly before spaces. PowerPoint ends a line with it (the line ends "slavery.“"). Euro-Office treats U+201C as CANT_BE_AT_END and glues the following space to it, so the quote moves to the next line and all later lines reflow (PowerPoint 21 lines, Euro-Office 22; SSIM 0.755). Slides (1): 154.

## What PowerPoint does

Quote stays at the end of the line; the spaces after it are the break opportunity.

## What Euro-Office does

Quote and following word move to the next line; the rest of the paragraph reflows.

## Suspected cause

g_aPunctuation[0x201C] has CANT_BE_AT_END, and Run.js keeps a para_Space after such an item instead of breaking there.

## Where to look

sdkjs/common/commonDefines.js:2340 (g_aPunctuation); sdkjs/word/Editor/Run.js ~4148

## Fix idea

Allow a break at a space that follows a CANT_BE_AT_END quote (PowerPoint's rule applies only when the quote is directly attached to the next word), at least in presentations.

## Open questions

- One occurrence; the rule for other opening punctuation followed by spaces was not tested.
