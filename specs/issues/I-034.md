## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Paragraphs have marL 18 pt and indent -26.64 pt, so the first line would start 8.64 pt left of the text inset. PowerPoint clamps it to the inset (x 19.2 pt); Euro-Office starts the first lines at x 10.56 pt, 8.64 pt too far left (SSIM 0.909). Slides (1): 2279.

## What PowerPoint does

First line starts at the left inset.

## What Euro-Office does

First line starts 8.64 pt left of the inset.

## Suspected cause

Paragraph_Recalculate.js adds Ind.Left + Ind.FirstLine to the line start with no clamp at 0.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph_Recalculate.js ~1740-1745

## Fix idea

In presentations, clamp Ind.Left + Ind.FirstLine to at least 0 for the first line.

## Open questions

- Whether PowerPoint clamps at 0 or at another bound for larger negative values was not tested.
