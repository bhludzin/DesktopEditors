## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). An incomplete fix: I-024. On lines without spc or a dash, glyphs still drift from PowerPoint's positions by 0.45-1.57 pt (0.5-1.7 px at 800 px) partway along the line, either at a word start or inside a word. Mostly Calibri and Calibri Bold, 14-28 pt (242 pages have such lines at 39 pt or more, 247 in body sizes). Includes lines ending in "?" (1978, 2692, 2693: -0.52 pt), Courier New on 2110 (+/-0.5 pt) and a misplaced kern after r and a right quote on 1425 (+1.1 pt). Most drifts are negative (Euro-Office short). 466 slides (listed in the review's triage.json), for example 3716, 3588, 3520, 3505, 75, 2575.

## What PowerPoint does

Word starts land on whole 600-dpi device pixels with per-run rounding.

## What Euro-Office does

The I-024 simulation reproduces only part of the lines; the remainder accumulates a different rounding.

## Suspected cause

I-024 device-placement simulation does not match PowerPoint's rounding for every run; the remaining error is per-run and depends on where the run starts.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Base.js; sdkjs TextShaper.js 498-517 and 710 (I-024/I-027 placement)

## Fix idea

Collect the worst lines (3716, 3588, 3520) and compare the simulated device positions with the PDF positions glyph by glyph to find which runs break the rule.

## Open questions

- The mechanism of the residual is not pinned down; it may be several small effects.
