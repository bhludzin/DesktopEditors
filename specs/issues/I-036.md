## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). An incomplete fix: I-027. On lines containing a hyphen or dash, the text after it drifts left of PowerPoint's position and the error grows along the line, up to 1.74 pt (3274, 16-pt Calibri). Measured 1.6-2.2 per mille short. 339 of 500 such lines are short, 161 long. 446 slides (listed in the review's triage.json), for example 3274, 1827, 3893, 3524, 2696, 1941.

## What PowerPoint does

Runs after the dash are placed on the 1/3000-inch grid with extra cells.

## What Euro-Office does

Runs after a dash get no extra cells, so they are short.

## Suspected cause

The I-027 rule "runs after a dash or mid-line space get no extra cells" does not match PowerPoint for this deck.

## Where to look

sdkjs TextShaper.js 498-517 and 710 (I-027 run grid)

## Fix idea

Give runs after a dash the same extra-cell treatment as other runs and re-measure 3274 and 1827.

## Open questions

- Some dash lines are long rather than short, so the rule may depend on run length or position.
