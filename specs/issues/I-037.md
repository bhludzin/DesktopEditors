# I-037: Runs with character spacing (spc) drift up to 1.9 pt: I-024 leaves them at their layout widths

## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). An incomplete fix: I-024. Lines with a:rPr spc drift from PowerPoint by up to 1.87 pt (2895, 27-pt Calibri; 3229 up to 1.77 pt). Most of these lines are long (365 of 515), the largest are short. 168 slides (listed in the review's triage.json), for example 2895, 3229, 3666, 1210, 2570, 531.

## What PowerPoint does

Applies device placement to spaced runs as to other runs.

## What Euro-Office does

spc runs were excluded from the I-024 device-placement fix.

## Suspected cause

The I-024 fix skips runs with spc, so their positions use the unrounded layout.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph/RunContent/Base.js and TextShaper.js (I-024 placement)

## Fix idea

Extend the device-placement rule to runs with spc, applying the spacing before rounding.

## Open questions

- How PowerPoint rounds spc itself was not measured separately.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
