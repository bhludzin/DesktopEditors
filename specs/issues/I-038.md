## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Shapes with scene3d (camera orthographicFront, lightRig threePt) and no sp3d are drawn by PowerPoint with a fill about 8-14 levels lighter than the stored colour; Euro-Office uses the stored colour. Slides (44): 2289-2293, 2299-2305, 2307-2310, 2330, 2332, 2336, 2363, 2366-2368, 2372-2374, 2389, 2391-2392, 2408-2409, 2412-2417, 2432, 2446-2448, 2450-2452.

## What PowerPoint does

Applies the three-point lighting to the flat face, which lightens it.

## What Euro-Office does

Plain fill colour.

## Suspected cause

No light is applied to fills. Since I-032 the scene3d is read (CSpPr.scene3d) and the front-face light is measured: each channel v becomes round(1.03894 v + 5.007), so 4472C4 is (76, 123, 209) and 808080 is 138; I-032 applies it to picture fills only.

## Where to look

sdkjs/common/Shapes/Serialize.js (spPr reader); shape fill drawing

## Fix idea

Apply getSlideSceneLighting (GraphicObjectBase.js) to solid and gradient fill colours, and decide on outlines and text (not measured).

## Open questions

- The lighting factor was measured only for threePt/t; it may depend on the colour.
