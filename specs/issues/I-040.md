## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Six CJK characters at 18 pt in runs tagged lang ja are drawn in SimSun by Euro-Office; PowerPoint uses MS PGothic (the theme Jpan font). Positions match; the glyph design differs (Song vs Gothic). Slides (1): 3830.

## What PowerPoint does

MS PGothic.

## What Euro-Office does

SimSun.

## Suspected cause

The theme font for the Jpan script (a:font script="Jpan") is not used for the run; the fallback picks a Chinese font.

## Where to look

sdkjs theme font resolution for East Asian runs (CTheme / font scheme script fonts)

## Fix idea

Resolve +mn-ea / +mj-ea by the run language (ja -> Jpan script font) before the generic fallback.

## Open questions

- The exact resolution path in sdkjs was not traced.
