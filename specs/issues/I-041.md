## What differs

Found by the 3963-slide deck's review (run i024-c, sdkjs f53e0a4a15). Line starts differ from PowerPoint by 0.3-0.5 pt on 61 pages (centred or right-aligned lines) and by about -0.12 pt on 72 pages (indented or bulleted lines). Sub-pixel at 800 px. 131 slides (listed in the review's triage.json), for example 3421, 3426, 314, 2965, 3527, 1510.

## What PowerPoint does

Line start after alignment rounded to its device grid.

## What Euro-Office does

Line start computed from the unrounded width.

## Suspected cause

Alignment offset uses the layout width, not the device-placed width; indents rounded differently.

## Where to look

sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph_Recalculate.js (line start / alignment)

## Fix idea

Compute the alignment offset from the device-placed line width.
