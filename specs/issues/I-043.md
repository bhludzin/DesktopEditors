# I-043: Saving a presentation drops its picture corrections (a14:imgProps, with their a14:imgLayer .wdp images)

## What differs

Found while checking I-042's fix, not by the review; not a rendering difference. A copy of the 3963-slide deck (great-roll-3963) saved from the desktop app loses all 774 of its a14:imgProps / a14:imgLayer picture corrections and the 234 .wdp files they point to; every PNG and JPEG picture is kept. The stock 9.3.8 release does the same (upstream behaviour, not a regression).

## What PowerPoint does

Writes a picture's corrections as a14:imgProps in the blip's extension list, with the original image as an a14:imgLayer (.wdp), so they can be changed or reset later; the blip itself is the corrected image.

## What Euro-Office does

Keeps the blip, the already-corrected image, so the slides should look the same after a save, but writes no a14:imgProps and none of the .wdp files, so PowerPoint can no longer change or reset those corrections.

## Suspected cause

Not traced. Neither core nor sdkjs reads a14:imgProps or a14:imgLayer: core reads a blip's extLst into PPTX::Logic::Ext, which keeps only svgBlip.

## Where to look

core OOXML/PPTXFormat/Logic/Fills/Blip.cpp (a blip's a:extLst) and OOXML/PPTXFormat/Logic/ExtP.cpp (PPTX::Logic::Ext, which reads and writes only svgBlip).

## Fix idea

Carry a14:imgProps and its .wdp parts through open and save, written back unchanged while the picture's image is not replaced.

## Open questions

- Not yet checked in PowerPoint: that the saved file looks the same there, and that its corrections can no longer be changed or reset.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
