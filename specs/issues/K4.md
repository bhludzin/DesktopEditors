# K4: Underline and strikethrough position and thickness are hard-coded instead of taken from the font's post/OS2 metrics (underline about 0.6 px high and 0.1 px thin; strike about 1 px high; bold strike not thicker)

## What differs

Underlines sit about 0.6 px higher and are about 0.1 px thinner than PowerPoint's (1600 px renders). Recorded as a slide tag.

## Suspected cause

ParagraphLineDrawState.updateStrikeoutUnderlinePos (word/Editor/Paragraph/draw/line-draw-state.js 544-569) places the strike at 0.27 em above the baseline. It places the underline at UnderlineOffset = 0.4 x line TextDescent (word/Editor/Paragraph.js 3169), and gives both the same thickness lineW = fontSize/18 pt. PowerPoint uses the face's own metrics: the underline top at post.underlinePosition with thickness about post.underlineThickness (Calibri -232/134 units), and the strike from OS/2 yStrikeoutPosition/yStrikeoutSize (Calibri 512/134, Calibri Bold 512/186). PDF page 125 (13.5 pt Calibri, baseline 342.86): PowerPoint fills the underline band 340.50-341.34, i.e. top 1.52 pt below the baseline (post predicts 1.53) and 0.84 pt thick (post 0.88). Euro-Office strokes 0.75 pt centred at 341.44, which is 0.52 pt (0.58 sheet px) higher and 0.09 pt (0.1 px) thinner: exactly the K4 tag. The strike in S0096-2 is 1 px too high and not thicker for bold, for the same reason.

## Also covers

Issues found separately that share this cause:

- Strikethrough uses a fixed 0.27 em position and fontSize/18 thickness instead of the font's OS/2 strikeout metrics (about 1 px too high, not thicker for bold) (code: sdkjs/word/Editor/Paragraph/draw/line-draw-state.js: ParagraphLineDrawState.updateStrikeoutUnderlinePos (544): `let strikeoutShift = 0.27;` (550) and `let lineW = (fontSize / 18) * g_dKoef_pt_to_mm;` (562))

Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
