# K5: Whole text lines up to 0.3 px higher or lower

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | fixed (fixed in sdkjs f1e24ab652 (fix(slide): place text baselines like PowerPoint)) |
| Severity | trivial |
| Slides | 558 (558 of them only through the slide tag K5) |
| Findings | 0 |
| Categories | text-position |
| Confidence in the cause | high |

## What differs

Whole lines sit 0.3 px or less higher or lower than PowerPoint's (1600 px renders). Recorded as a slide tag.

## What PowerPoint does

Puts each line's baseline at a whole number of 1/600 inch (0.12 pt) below the top of the text, halves rounded down. The top of the text is where the first line starts: the text area's top after tIns, moved by the anchoring (ctr, b). The whole distance is rounded, not its parts: the first line's ascent part (Calibri 0.78 x 1.2 x size), space before and line spacing included. Calibri 13.5 pt: 12.636 -> 12.6 pt, 18 pt: 16.848 -> 16.8, 12 pt: 11.232 -> 11.28; the fourth line of 13.5 pt at lnSpc 93%, 472.5 steps down, at 472. The PDF then stretches text positions down by 0.1075 pt over the slide's height about its top edge (depth y is drawn at y x (1 + 0.1075 pt / slide height)) and puts them on a 1/3000-inch (0.024 pt) grid counted from the slide's top. In a rotated box the rounding is in the box's own frame and the stretch and grid in slide coordinates. Horizontally there is a similar stretch, left as I-024.

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: drew each line at its exact layout baseline, so lines sat up to 0.18 pt above or below PowerPoint's (0.13 pt at the 99th percentile): 12.7% of the deck's lines were on PowerPoint's baseline and 60.7% within one 0.024 pt step.

## Suspected cause

Confirmed. PowerPoint keeps baselines on a 1/600-inch grid counted from the top of the text, and its PDF export stretches and snaps text positions; Euro-Office drew the glyphs at the exact layout positions.

## Where to look

sdkjs word/Editor/Paragraph.js: drawRunContentElements calls GetSlideBaselineInfo (the DocContent's top, the text transform, the slide height) and GetSlideLinePos (SLIDE_BASELINE_GRID, SLIDE_TEXT_STRETCH, SLIDE_TEXT_POSITION_GRID).

## Fix idea

Done in the presentation editor: the glyphs of each line of slide text are drawn at PowerPoint's baseline, rounded from the DocContent's top, then stretched and snapped in slide coordinates. The layout, line breaks, hit testing and the underlines and strikes (K4) are unchanged.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
