# K6: Pictures are written to PDF as tiling-pattern fills instead of clipped image draws, so they are resampled more smoothly than PowerPoint's and their edge rows/columns are under-painted

## What differs

A picture in the same place and size is interpolated slightly more smoothly than PowerPoint's. Recorded as a slide tag.

## What PowerPoint does

Draws each picture as an image XObject clipped to its shape ('q <path> W* n  w 0 0 h x y cm /ImageN Do Q'), one XObject per medium (and per crop) shared by every page that shows it. A cropped picture's image holds only the part shown (srcRect l 10%, t 20%, r 5%, b 15% of a 320 x 200 picture: a 272 x 130 image). Every picture is re-encoded, a JPEG too: lossless (Flate of the RGB, no predictor, /Interpolate false) when it has at most 256 colours or when JPEG would not make it smaller, otherwise JPEG at quality 75 (the Windows codec: entropy data identical to GDI+ at quality 75, plus a 92-byte Exif segment; 4:2:0, /Interpolate true). Transparency (the picture's alpha times alphaModFix) is a soft mask (Flate, DeviceGray, /Matte [0 0 0]) with the colour premultiplied by it. Images drawn on their own (the blurred shadows of I-014) follow the same rule. Tiled picture fills are tiling patterns, one tile per step. Pictures drawn above about 270 dpi are downsampled (I-025).

## What Euro-Office does

Before the fix: painted each picture as a path filled with a tiling pattern (PatternType 1, XStep and YStep twice the picture's size, a transparent margin of half its width and height around the image), which Windows' renderer resampled on the tile's own grid: the picture came out smoother than PowerPoint's and its edge rows and columns partly transparent (slide 37's column 799 at 800 px: 239 against PowerPoint's 81). Every picture that was not a JPEG became a JPEG at quality 85 (IJG tables), a JPEG was passed through and a cropped picture was embedded whole. Now, for slides, drawn and encoded as PowerPoint does, with libjpeg at quality 75 (sizes within about 1% of PowerPoint's).

## Suspected cause

Confirmed: core's PDF writer, not sdkjs. sdkjs sends a picture as a path filled with a texture brush over the picture's rect; CPdfWriter::UpdateBrush turned every texture brush into a tiling pattern, and CPdfWriter::LoadImage encoded every picture as a JPEG at quality 85.

## Where to look

core PdfFile/PdfWriter.cpp: UpdateBrush calls UpdateSlidePictureBrush (a stretched picture's image and rect, cropped to the path's bounds), which DrawPath draws under a clip to the path before stroking; LoadSlideImage encodes the pictures, and DrawImage uses it for images drawn on their own. Turned on only for presentations: CPdfFile::SetPresentationMode (PdfFile.h/.cpp, CPdfWriter::SetPresentationMode), called by x2t's doct_bin2pdf (X2tConverter/src/lib/pdf_image.h) when the binary is a presentation (PPTT).

## Fix idea

Done in core, for presentations only (documents and spreadsheets are unchanged): stretched pictures drawn as images clipped to their shape, cropped to what the shape shows, and encoded as PowerPoint encodes them. Tiled fills stay patterns, as in PowerPoint's PDF. Not copied: downsampling above about 270 dpi (I-025) and the Windows JPEG codec (libjpeg at quality 75 instead: the same tables, slightly different rounding).

## Also covers

Issues found separately that share this cause:

- Pictures are exported as tiling-pattern fills, so their edge rows and columns are under-painted (code: sdkjs word/Drawing/ShapeDrawer.js: drawFillStroke (line 2264). Its isPdf() branch sets the texture brush at 2311-2366 (put_brushTexture 2320/2336, put_TextureBounds 2338/2365) and fills with drawpath(256) at 2545. It is called from common/Drawings/Format/Image.js: CImageShape.prototype.draw (lines 642-643). The renderer side is common/Drawings/Metafile.js: CDocumentRenderer.prototype.put_brushTexture (3388). CDocumentRenderer.prototype.drawImage (3278) and StartClipPath/EndClipPath (3477/3485) already exist and write ctDrawImageFromFile. The pattern itself is built in core's PdfWriter (not sdkjs; not located).)

## Open questions

- After the direct-draw rewrite a residual of about 1 level remains (max 23). On page 106 Euro-Office embeds the PNG picture (media/image14.png) as DCTDecode (JPEG), while PowerPoint keeps it lossless (FlateDecode, /Interpolate false). That lossy re-encode is probably in core's PDF writer (x2t), not in sdkjs, and is a separate small issue.
- Two of the deck's 65 media that PowerPoint keeps at full size do not follow the encoding rule: image54 (29 x 27, 547 colours with alpha: Flate 1721 bytes against JPEG 906) and image34 (116 x 173, 2105 colours: Flate 10265 against JPEG 2855) are lossless in PowerPoint's PDF, and Euro-Office writes them as JPEG. Their slide XML is like the others' (image57, also with a backgroundRemoval effect, is a JPEG in both), and no colour count or size ratio separates them from image44 (2470 colours, Flate 3.5 times the JPEG: a JPEG in both).

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
