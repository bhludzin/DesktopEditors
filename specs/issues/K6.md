# K6: Pictures are written to PDF as tiling-pattern fills instead of clipped image draws, so they are resampled more smoothly than PowerPoint's and their edge rows/columns are under-painted

## What differs

A picture in the same place and size is interpolated slightly more smoothly than PowerPoint's. Recorded as a slide tag.

## Suspected cause

In PDF/renderer mode, CImageShape.draw sends the blip through ShapeDrawer.drawFillStroke's isPdf() branch as a texture brush (put_brushTexture + put_TextureBounds + drawpath(256), ShapeDrawer.js 2311-2366/2545). The PDF writer emits it as a PatternType 1 tiling pattern whose BBox adds a transparent half-width margin. PowerPoint instead draws a clipped image XObject ('re W n ... cm /Image Do'). Windows.Data.Pdf rasterizes the pattern tile on its own grid and resamples it into the page. The picture content therefore comes out smoother than PowerPoint's directly drawn (pixel-replicated or snapped) image (K6), and the outer row or column blends with the tile's transparent margin (the under-painted edges of pictures-misc/1).

## Also covers

Issues found separately that share this cause:

- Pictures are exported as tiling-pattern fills, so their edge rows and columns are under-painted (code: sdkjs word/Drawing/ShapeDrawer.js: drawFillStroke (line 2264). Its isPdf() branch sets the texture brush at 2311-2366 (put_brushTexture 2320/2336, put_TextureBounds 2338/2365) and fills with drawpath(256) at 2545. It is called from common/Drawings/Format/Image.js: CImageShape.prototype.draw (lines 642-643). The renderer side is common/Drawings/Metafile.js: CDocumentRenderer.prototype.put_brushTexture (3388). CDocumentRenderer.prototype.drawImage (3278) and StartClipPath/EndClipPath (3477/3485) already exist and write ctDrawImageFromFile. The pattern itself is built in core's PdfWriter (not sdkjs; not located).)

## Open questions

- After the direct-draw rewrite a residual of about 1 level remains (max 23). On page 106 Euro-Office embeds the PNG picture (media/image14.png) as DCTDecode (JPEG), while PowerPoint keeps it lossless (FlateDecode, /Interpolate false). That lossy re-encode is probably in core's PDF writer (x2t), not in sdkjs, and is a separate small issue.

## How to verify

Build and render with `python fidelity/fidelity.py loop <deck> --label <name>`, then rebuild the review facts with `python fidelity/review_prep.py <deck-dir> <name>` and look at the slides below: their findings should be gone, and no other slide should lose line-break matches or SSIM against the previous run (`fidelity.py runs <deck>`). Then set `status` and `fixed_in` in `issues.json`, add a `log` entry, and re-run `review_record.py`.
